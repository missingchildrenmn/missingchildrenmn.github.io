Use ContentCard to group a record's fields or a piece of guidance — it is the app's `.content-card` and the most brand-identifying container MCM has.

```jsx
<ContentCard title="Physical details" actions={<Button variant="icon" icon="create-outline" />}>
  <p>Height, weight, eye color…</p>
</ContentCard>
```

The offset teal shadow is deliberate and un-blurred; do not swap it for a soft drop shadow.
